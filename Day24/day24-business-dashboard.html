Sustainability verdict

On current evidence the business is not yet sustainable: no paying customer, small consumer revenue at realistic reach, and an institutional route with a verified awareness hook but an unproven budget.

Validate next: students act on a sourced plan, one college runs a paid or formal pilot, and trustworthy data can be maintained.
