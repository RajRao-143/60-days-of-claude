How to Play

  • Each round lasts 3 minutes. Incidents arrive faster as time passes and several can be open at once.
  • Every incident has a deadline. Pick one response; the outcome depends on the incident type and its priority.
  • Delay decision buys 15 extra seconds once, with a small penalty. Ignore is costly for Medium+ priorities.
  • Emergency actions (air freight, backup suppliers) protect service but raise operating cost. Overspending on low-priority alerts is penalised.
  • Cheap "wait it out" choices often trigger delayed consequences or follow-up incidents.
  • Score = KPI health + revenue protected + good decisions − poor decisions, expired alerts and budget overruns.
  • Shortcuts: P pause/resume, M sound, H help, Esc close. All buttons are keyboard focusable.
