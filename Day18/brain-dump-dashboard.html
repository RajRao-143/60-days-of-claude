Summary

The CEO opened by stating that Q2 revenue grew 12%, below the 18% target committed to at the start of the year. The Head of Sales attributed most of the gap to enterprise deals slipping into next quarter: 14 large contracts were expected to close and 9 were finalized. Procurement delays were the biggest reason, and two clients requested additional security reviews before signing.

Marketing reported website traffic up 34% compared to last quarter with conversion rates still lower than expected, and a planned website redesign. The Product Director and CTO raised engineering capacity, with the analytics dashboard scheduled for August. Customer Success reported 94% retention and a significant rise in support ticket volume tied to reporting performance and dashboard loading times. The CEO deferred spending, hiring and conference budget decisions, asked for no public commitments until the engineering milestone review is complete, and said open items will be followed up at next week's leadership meeting.
