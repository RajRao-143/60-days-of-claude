How this works

Purpose: Media Integrity Analyzer teaches media literacy by exploring fictional examples. It is not a quiz on what you already know.

  • Challenge 1 — Headline Detective: decide whether you would click a headline, read the matching article, flag possible warning signs, and judge how well the two match.
  • Challenge 2 — Emotion Detector: notice how a fictional post makes you feel, pick the phrases and techniques you think shape that reaction, and guess the audience.
  • When analysis appears: only after you press “Reveal Analysis”. Nothing is revealed early.
  • Metrics: Headline Accuracy, Source Reliability, Emotional Manipulation and Audience Targeting start at a baseline of 50 and move based on your choices. Mistakes are learning moments, and strong feelings are never penalized.
  • The score is educational: it reflects the analysis you demonstrated in this session. It is not a measure of intelligence, and this app cannot tell whether real-world news is true. It teaches you to inspect evidence, wording, context and persuasion.
  • Replay: on the final dashboard choose “Replay with New Scenarios” for fresh fictional examples and a clean score.

Fictional scenarios: every headline, article and post is invented and generated locally. Nothing is fetched from the internet.
