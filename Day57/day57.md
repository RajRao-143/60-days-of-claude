Day 57 — Interview Simulator & Evaluation

🎯 Objective

Build a text-first mock interview simulator in LaunchPath AI to help students and freshers prepare for HR and technical interviews through structured questions, answer evaluation, and actionable feedback.

🚀 Features Implemented

1. Interview Presets

- Quick 5 — Short interview practice.
- Standard 10 — Regular interview preparation.
- Deep 15–20 — Detailed interview practice.
- Custom — User-defined interview length.

2. Role-Based Interview Questions

- Curated HR and technical questions.
- Questions organized by role, topic, and difficulty.
- Interview questions designed for structured practice.

3. Text-Based Interview Experience

- Capture answers directly in the application.
- Display current question and session progress.
- Support completing an interview without paid AI APIs.
- Maintain text-based practice as the primary interview mode.

4. Transparent Evaluation Rubric

Answers are evaluated using five dimensions:

1. Relevance — How well the answer addresses the question.
2. Structure — How clearly the answer is organized.
3. Correctness — Accuracy of the technical or conceptual explanation.
4. Clarity — How understandable the response is.
5. Examples — Use of relevant examples to support the answer.

5. Actionable Feedback

- Feedback based on the submitted answer.
- One identified strength.
- One area for improvement.
- One actionable practice task.

6. Responsible Interview Evaluation

- No personality scoring.
- No accent or emotion evaluation.
- No protected-trait assessment.
- No hiring-probability predictions.
- Text mode remains available when voice functionality is unsupported.

- CSS3

⚠️ Implementation Notes & Limitations

- The interview simulator uses curated questions and structured evaluation.
- No paid external AI API is required for the core experience.
- Feedback should be interpreted as practice guidance, not a professional hiring assessment.
- Voice support is optional and must never block text interviews.
- Live deployment and end-to-end verification remain pending.
