# Day 6 – Resume Optimization & ATS

## 🎯 Objective

The goal of Day 6 was to use Claude to analyze my existing resume
for ATS parsing and recruiter readability and generate an optimized
one-page resume.

## 📄 Original Resume

I uploaded my existing resume to Claude and used the provided
Resume Optimizer prompt.

![Original Resume](./day6-original-resume.png)

## 📊 ATS Analysis

### Claude-Generated ATS Score

- Previous Score: 58/100
- Optimized Score: 88/100

### Key Improvements

- Rewrote the internship information into a clearer job title,
  company, and date format.
- Expanded SIF to Serious Injury & Fatality (SIF) for better
  keyword matching.
- Converted dense content into consistent action-verb bullets.
- Standardized section headings to ATS-friendly labels.
- Grouped technical skills into clear categories for better parsing.
- Removed ATS-unfriendly elements such as tables, icons, columns,
  and text boxes.
- Improved the Professional Summary with relevant technical keywords.
- Standardized dates, punctuation, spacing, and formatting.

![ATS Analysis](./day6-ats-analysis.png)

## 🚀 Optimized Resume

Claude generated a one-page A4 resume using a single-column,
ATS-friendly format focused on recruiter readability.

![Optimized Resume](./day6-optimized-resume.png)

## 💡 Key Learnings

- ATS optimization requires a clear and consistent resume structure.
- Standard section headings can make resume information easier to parse.
- Relevant keywords should be included naturally and truthfully.
- Strong action verbs make project and experience descriptions clearer.
- Simple formatting is more suitable for ATS parsing.
- Resume optimization should improve readability without inventing
  achievements, skills, or metrics.
- AI can help identify formatting and content improvements while
  keeping the original information truthful.
