Section 1

The real decision

You are choosing between the shortest path to the engineer you want to become (A) and the widest door to a first offer (B).

The trade-off is long-term fit against near-term placement security.

It feels hard because your gut already points to A, and your fear of missing placements is louder than your evidence.

Section 2

Case for each option

Option A · AI/ML + Cloud

Strongest case for

It is the goal you named. Two or three deployed ML projects give an internship reviewer something specific to judge.

Hidden upside

Cloud skills turn notebooks into running services. A live demo separates you from tutorial-only portfolios.

Biggest weakness

DSA and placement prep get no built-in schedule, so they are the first thing to slip.

Best if you value

Depth in the field you want over the number of open doors.

Option B · Full-Stack + DSA

Strongest case for

DSA and web skills are screened in the widest range of internship and placement processes. It is the lowest-risk route to interviews.

Hidden upside

A shipped full-stack app is a ready-made home for AI features later. Fundamentals transfer.

Biggest weakness

It can quietly postpone the AI/ML goal until "later" never comes.

Best if you value

Security and a clear checklist over specialization.

Section 3

Assumption buster

3 assumptions to test
  • Assumption"AI/ML internships don't test DSA."Many companies run coding rounds for every engineering role. Check 10 real AI/ML postings before believing either way.
  • Assumption"Choosing B means giving up AI/ML."It delays AI/ML. A full-stack project with an ML feature keeps the goal alive inside B.
  • Assumption"The next 6 months is one all-or-nothing bet."You said the decision is reversible. A check-in at month 2 and month 4 lowers the stakes further.
2 cognitive biases at work
  • Loss aversionMissing a placement feels larger than the equal gain of building the exact skills you want, so fear is outvoting the goal.
  • Confirmation biasWith a gut preference for A, you may collect only evidence that supports A. Include the strongest evidence for B in the 7-day test.
1 overlooked factor
  • Your placement calendar.The real deadline is the date of the first coding screening you want to clear, not "6 months". I don't know that date. Finding it on Day 2 is the single most useful fact you can add.

Section 5

Premortem

Imagine it is 12 months from now and this choice failed.

If A failed

3 reasons
  1. Tutorial hopping across too many tools, with no finished project.
  2. DSA faded, and coding rounds filtered you out.
  3. Projects looked like copies of common tutorials and had no live demo.
Warning sign

Month 2 arrives with zero deployed projects, or three straight weeks with no DSA practice.

Prevention

Set a weekly DSA floor in your calendar and ship one live demo by week 8.

If B failed

3 reasons
  1. You ended up with a generic CRUD portfolio nobody remembers.
  2. DSA grinding crowded out ML, and motivation dropped.
  3. You had no AI/ML signal when AI/ML internships opened.
Warning sign

You dread sessions and keep opening ML content on the side for several weeks.

Prevention

Block 3 hours a week for an ML build, or add an AI feature to your full-stack project.

Section 6

7-day test plan

One action a day. Thresholds are proposals you can change.

  1. Day 1Research

    Collect 10 AI/ML and 10 SDE internship postings and tally how often DSA, cloud and ML projects are required.

  2. Day 2Research

    Find your placement calendar and eligibility rules, and note the date of the earliest coding screening.

  3. Day 3Experiment

    Build a tiny end-to-end ML demo (train, wrap in an API, deploy on a free cloud tier) and note what energized or frustrated you.

  4. Day 4Experiment

    Solve 4 easy-to-medium DSA problems against a timer and record the time and where you got stuck.

  5. Day 5Talk

    Message 2 seniors or alumni who landed AI/ML internships and ask how they balanced DSA with projects.

  6. Day 6Talk

    Ask a placement coordinator or recruiter what their coding rounds screen for, and how much a project portfolio counts.

  7. Day 7Decide

    Confirm A if the demo excited you, postings reward projects, and about 5 DSA hours a week fits. Switch to B if the first screening is under 12 weeks away and your timed solving is weak.

Section 7

The verdict

Choose Option A: AI/ML + Cloud, with a fixed DSA floor.
Why

It leads the matrix, it matches your stated goal, and your biggest fear has a direct fix. Your fear is about placements, and that is a scheduling problem you can solve inside A with a weekly DSA block, without switching paths.

What could change this verdict
  • Your first coding screening is within roughly 12 weeks and your timed DSA results are weak.
  • The Day 1 postings show AI/ML roles mostly ask for DSA and little project work.
  • You cannot hold the weekly DSA floor for two weeks in a row.
Hard truthA strong AI/ML portfolio will not save you from a coding round you didn't practice for.
