Architect's reminder

Start with the problem, not the model. A simple baseline and reliable evaluation are more useful than a complex model with no evidence.

Estimate: 1–3 hrsOutput: Evaluation report

Tasks

Tools & practices

AI prompt to use

Interpret these evaluation results for [task]: [metrics and confusion matrix]. Explain what each metric means, identify likely failure modes, discuss threshold trade-offs and potential bias, and recommend further tests. Separate observations from hypotheses and do not infer causation from feature importance alone.

Quick decision guide

Do you have labeled data?

Consider supervised learning when labels are reliable and match the target you need to predict.

No reliable labels?

Explore unsupervised methods, anomaly detection, rules, or collecting labels. Choose based on the use case.

Is the dataset small?

Start with simple baselines and cross-validation where appropriate. Avoid unnecessarily complex models.

Will people rely on predictions?

Assess error costs, uncertainty, privacy, bias, human review, and failure handling before deployment.
