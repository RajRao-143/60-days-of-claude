Day 50 – Defend Your Experience 🎯

Project: Defend Your Experience – AI Interview Defense Simulator

Day 50 of the 60 Days of Claude Challenge

📌 Overview

Defend Your Experience is an AI-powered interview preparation simulator designed to help students and job seekers confidently defend every claim, skill, and project mentioned in their resumes.

Instead of simply rewriting a resume, the platform challenges users with realistic HR and technical interview questions based on their actual experience.

✨ Key Features

- Experience-Based Questions: Generates interview questions from the user's profile, skills, and projects.
- HR & Technical Interviews: Supports both behavioral and technical interview preparation.
- Adaptive Difficulty: Adjusts question difficulty based on the user's responses.
- Claim Detection: Identifies experience claims that may need stronger explanations or supporting evidence.
- Answer Evaluation: Provides feedback, confidence indicators, and improvement suggestions.
- Defense Report: Summarizes strengths, weak areas, and preparation recommendations.
- Session History: Saves previous interview sessions for progress tracking.
- Export Options: Export interview reports and session data.
- Responsive Dashboard: Provides a clean, premium interface across screen sizes.

⚙️ How It Works

1. Add or upload your resume and experience details.
2. The platform identifies key skills, projects, and claims.
3. Start an HR or technical interview session.
4. Answer questions based on your actual experience.
5. Receive feedback and identify areas that need improvement.
6. Review your Defense Report and track your progress.

🎯 Objective

The goal is simple: Don't just put skills and projects on your resume — be ready to defend them.

This project focuses on building interview confidence, improving technical communication, and preparing for real-world internship and placement interviews.

💡 Key Learning

This challenge helped me explore interactive AI-powered application development, dynamic question generation, response evaluation, browser-based data persistence, and building a practical interview preparation experience.
